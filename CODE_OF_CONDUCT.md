# Code of Conduct

LuckyDrop is committed to a welcoming, respectful community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different cultures, identities, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, never attacks on the person.
- Credit research and creative work appropriately.
- Take particular care when discussing cultural or religious symbols.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through
GitHub's available private reporting or moderation tools. Do not disclose
sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers,
reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing LuckyDrop.
